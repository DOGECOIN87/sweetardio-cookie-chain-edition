# Cookie Chain Edition: Reference-Faithful Replacement Composition

The replacement adopts the original Sweetardio.fun landing page’s composition rather than borrowing only its color palette.

## Page Sequence

| Sequence | Role | Content limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero arcade panel** | Establish identity and primary action | Plaque, one status chip, Sweetardio / Cookie Chain wordmark, one short statement, and one mint action. |
| **Aisle 01: The Draw** | Let visitors inspect the collection artwork | One selected full-quality artwork, its token number, name, and rarity; compact user-controlled selector. |
| **Aisle 02: The Mint** | Provide the transaction interface | Terminal status, quantity, price, wallet entry/mint control, and only safety messages needed for the current state. |
| **Footer** | Close the scene | Brand lockup, collection copyright, and one back-to-top link. |

## Material and Type Rules

The shop-scene background is visible at the sides of the centered hero panel through a muted Oxford overlay. The hero panel has a thin cerise-to-cyan edge and a dark glass fill. Pusia is used only for the Sweetardio wordmark, collection title, artwork name, and mint heading. DM Mono is used for small status language and control labels. Buttons and selection controls use square arcade geometry, but no duplicated neon glows, card grids, or decorative data lines.

## Responsive Rules

At desktop widths the hero panel remains centered and bounded, with the scene visible around it. At mobile widths, the panel keeps a clear vertical order and the mint action remains immediately after the value statement. The artwork selector remains user-controlled, uses horizontal overflow only where necessary, and never auto-rotates.

## Review Notes

The 1365×768 replacement capture now follows the original landing page’s centered, gradient-edged panel hierarchy: the gold Cookie Chain plaque, status chip, wordmark, one short statement, and one directional mint control are grouped as a single scene object. At 390×844, the same hierarchy remains intact without compressed labels or an overfilled first screen; the draw aisle begins naturally below the panel.
