# Cookie Chain Edition: Design Upgrade Contract

The upgrade keeps the current Sweetardio.fun arcade system rather than replacing it. It preserves the canonical Cookie Chain Edition plaque, Oxford Blue shop-scene background, Pusia display treatment, Candy Machine safeguards, and collection-specific asset boundaries.

| Enhancement | Purpose | Visible change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Candy-counter marquee** | Make the collection’s sticker and trait vocabulary feel like a physical shop display. | A layered moving ticker gives the transition below the hero a stronger arcade-machine rhythm. |
| **Hero cabinet lighting** | Strengthen the original site’s illuminated-machine motif without obscuring the canonical gold plaque. | A perimeter bulb rail and restrained cyan/cerise bloom frame the hero console. |
| **Draw-deck rail** | Make featured-art selection read as a curated rack rather than a row of tiny controls. | Token selector buttons become a legible tape-strip with active draw status. |
| **Mint-terminal feedback** | Give the quantity, price, and safety state a coherent operator-console hierarchy. | Focus states, state labels, and status treatment become more tactile and scannable. |
| **Mobile density pass** | Preserve the storefront atmosphere on narrow screens rather than simply stacking desktop modules. | The hero plaque, counter controls, draw deck, and section transitions retain intentional scale and spacing. |

The implementation must use these decisions as a single visual system. It must not change wallet connection, Candy Machine configuration, Candy Guard validation, treasury matching, supply facts, trait definitions, or the Vite preview-host policy.

## Validation Notes

The desktop preview confirms that the canonical gold Cookie Chain Edition plaque remains the hero focal point. The new bulb rails visibly frame the glass cabinet without reducing wordmark contrast, while the ticker, draw deck, and staged terminal meter are present and legible.
